FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products for human consumption?
All Body Pharma products are labeled "For Research Use Only" and "Not for Human Consumption." They are intended solely for laboratory and in-vitro research applications by qualified professionals.
How do I verify product purity?
Every batch includes a Certificate of Analysis (COA) from an independent, accredited third-party laboratory. COA documents are available directly on each product page and provided with every shipment, detailing purity levels, testing methodology, and batch-specific results.
What is your shipping policy?
Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship same day. We offer standard (3-5 business days), expedited (2 business days), and overnight shipping. All orders ship in discreet, unmarked packaging with temperature indicators. International shipping available to select countries.
How should peptides be stored?
Lyophilized peptides should be stored at -20°C for long-term stability. Reconstituted peptides should be stored at 4°C and used within 30 days. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles. Specific storage recommendations are included with each product.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers for orders over $500. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption and are PCI-DSS compliant.
Do you offer bulk pricing for research institutions?
Yes. We offer institutional pricing for universities, research hospitals, and corporate laboratories. Volume discounts begin at orders over $1,000. Contact our research team at research@bodypharma.com for a custom quote and to set up a net-30 account.
